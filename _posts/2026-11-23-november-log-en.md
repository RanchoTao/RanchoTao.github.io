My last note was in mid-October. I did a fair amount of work since then, but most of it stayed in repositories and chat logs. This is a short catch-up; I will write up the parts worth expanding separately.

## Paper

A submitted paper came back with reviews, and I finished the rebuttal. The reviews were not encouraging. I will revise based on them and submit to the next conference.

It was a reminder that external evaluation, uncomfortable as it is, is the most useful feedback there is.

## Visual Deadline

I rewrote the task-ranking model in VD: priority = importance × urgency, where urgency comes from "remaining work ÷ remaining available time" rather than days until the deadline. The model broke three times during design, and each failure is now a regression test.

Details: [Importance × Urgency: Rewriting the Ranking Model in Visual Deadline](/posts/2026/11/importance-times-urgency/en/).

## Professor Yau's world of mathematics

I built a website introducing Professor Shing-Tung Yau's mathematical work: [masteryau.com](https://masteryau.com). It is an unofficial personal project.

The next idea is to give each major theorem on the site a bilingual (Chinese/English) page that explains, from scratch, what the original paper does, so interested readers can work through it themselves.

## Homepage

I cleaned up the [projects page](/projects/) to four projects that best represent my work: Visual Deadline, RanchoResearch, Ripple-Quant, and the Yau site. The others are not without value; they just do not all need to be on the front page.

## Pruning the list

I listed everything I am doing or want to do, got nearly two hundred items, and cut them down to three lines: study, career, and health. The main finding: my problem is not a lack of time, but ordering and closure.

Written up here: [Everything Looks Good in the Cart: Pruning My List](/posts/2026/11/pruning-the-list/en/).

## In progress

- **Final exams**: several finals in early December. That is the top priority for the next two weeks.
- **TOEFL**: aiming for 100+ as soon as possible.

## Last word

This log is itself a reminder: if I do not write things down, after a while even I forget what I did. I will try to write one each month.
