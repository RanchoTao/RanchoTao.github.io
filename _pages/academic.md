## Current Directions

I currently focus on four connected areas:

- Learning theory and model behavior
- Quantitative trading and sequential decision-making
- Cognitive augmentation and human–AI collaboration
- AI agents / research harnesses

## Ongoing Research

### Training-Stage Behavior and Interpretability of Language Models

I study layer-level behavior, intervention responses, and cross-checkpoint stability across stages of pretraining, with an emphasis on reproducible experimental design, causal interventions, and auditable results.

### Identification of Heavy-Tailed Stochastic Processes

I am studying random time changes, subordination, heavy-tailed noise, and data-driven identification of non-Gaussian stochastic processes.

### Research Harnesses

I explore how AI agents can organize literature review, problem decomposition, experiments, code auditing, result verification, and paper writing into traceable and testable research workflows.
